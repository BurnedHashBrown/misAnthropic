About Filtr.

Romance should not be treated like a risk.

Filtr. exists because genuine connection and manipulation can look similar at first. The difference often arrives later, when care turns into control.

We do not want to police every long-distance relationship. We want to give people a little more space when secrecy, urgency, intimate-image pressure, or money requests start to gather.

That pause can protect a choice, a relationship with a trusted person, and a future self.
