# Filtr. design context

## Brand

Name: Filtr.

Palette:

- Amber Flame `#ffbc0d`
- Tomato `#fe4a49`
- Grapefruit Pink `#ff7070`
- Onyx `#0b090c`
- Porcelain `#fdfffc`

## Visual direction

The visual language is bold editorial safety, inspired by the existing Paperfolio-style direction: oversized sans-serif typography, hairline rules, asymmetric space, flat blocks of color, near-square corners, and one clear action at a time. It avoids sterile security-product visuals and avoids treating the user as a suspect.

## Interaction direction

- Arrow-fill buttons communicate primary actions.
- The auto-scroll signal rail communicates ongoing context and pauses on hover.
- Notifications are reserved for meaningful escalation, never routine romance.
- The uploader previews files locally in the browser.
- Toggle navigation collapses on mobile.
- Forms use visible labels, inline demo feedback, and high contrast.

## Responsive rules

- Navigation becomes a toggle below 620px.
- Multi-column layouts collapse to one column below 900px.
- Tap targets remain at least 39px tall.
- Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
