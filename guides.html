Safety guides

Clear words for complicated moments.

Practical guides for users, parents, and trusted adults. Start with the guide that fits your situation.

A healthy connection leaves room for your boundaries. Pressure often uses urgency, guilt, secrecy, or a sudden crisis to make your choice feel smaller.

Lead with care, not confiscation. Ask what feels different, make it safe to share mistakes, and focus on the behaviour rather than blaming the person.

Listen first. Help save evidence, make a pause plan, and stay with them while they report or contact support.

Use a separate, independent channel. Do not rely on a profile photo, a story, or a rushed video call alone.
