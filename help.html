Emergency help

Do not negotiate with pressure.

If someone is threatening, blackmailing, or pressuring you, this is a moment to pause and get another person beside you.

1

Stop sending

Do not send money, images, codes, IDs, or more messages to make the pressure stop.

2

Save evidence

Keep usernames, messages, payment details, and threats. Use the Evidence Locker if it feels safe.

3

Tell someone

Contact a trusted adult, friend, school support person, or local victim-support service.

4

Report and block

Report the account on the platform after saving the evidence. Block once you have your plan.

If there is immediate danger, contact local emergency services. Filtr. is not an emergency service.
