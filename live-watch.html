Architecture & Safety

How Live Watch protects you without invading your space.

Built from the ground up for restraint and strict privacy. Filtr. never monitors your personal life to judge you — it exists only to give you breathing room when manipulation starts to compound.

Pillar 01

Side-Panel Presence

Live Watch docks natively in your browser's side panel alongside WhatsApp Web. You don't need to copy and paste suspicious messages into another window — it gently observes incoming chat text in real time.

Pillar 02

100% On-Device & Zero Uploads

Your conversations never leave your computer. The entire pattern engine executes inside your browser's local sandbox memory. Zero server uploads, zero chat log storage, and zero tracking.

Pillar 03

Restraint & Cumulative Patterns

Affection, distance, compliments, and daily frequency are never flagged. Filtr. only responds when high-risk signals compound over time: secrecy requests + urgency + financial demands + private-image pressure.

Pillar 04

Calm, Non-Judgmental Guidance

No alarming red sirens or accusatory labels. Live Watch offers balanced observations, ready-to-copy pause messages to stall pressure gracefully, and direct links to trusted support resources.

Evaluation Matrix

Three calibrated states, never premature alarm.

Romance scams and extortion prey on sudden isolation. Here is how Live Watch categorizes patterns as they develop:

Safe · Level 0

New Connection

Nudge: Enjoy the conversation at your own pace.
Watch · Level 1

How to Install the .ZIP Package (Manual Sideload)

Follow these 5 steps to install Filtr. Live Watch manually in Microsoft Edge, Google Chrome, or Brave:

1
Download & Extract Archive Download the filtr-extension.zip file above and extract its contents to a folder on your computer (e.g. Downloads/filtr-extension).
2
Open Browser Extensions Manager In your browser URL bar, navigate to the extensions settings page:
edge://extensions or for Chrome/Brave: chrome://extensions
3
Enable Developer Mode Turn on the Developer mode toggle switch (located in the left sidebar in Edge, or top-right corner in Chrome).
4

5

Permissions & Transparency

Clear answers about your privacy.

Does Live Watch break WhatsApp's encryption?

No. Live Watch does not touch network packets or cryptographic keys. It operates solely in your browser window as a reader to detect specific manipulation strings locally after the messages have already been decrypted and rendered for you.

Are my chats sent to any AI cloud servers?

Never. Unlike cloud-based chatbots, Live Watch's pattern recognition engine runs entirely on your own processor inside pattern-engine.js. No logs or text snippets are ever transmitted outside your computer.

Can I pause or disable it at any time?

Yes. The side panel contains an instant "Pause" button that halts all DOM observation with a single click. You remain in complete control of when it runs.

Need to check an existing message?

You can also paste messages for a one-time check.
