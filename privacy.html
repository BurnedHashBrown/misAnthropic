Privacy

Your messages are not our product.

Privacy is part of safety. This prototype is designed around local review, deliberate sharing, and clear limits.

Local by default

Paste Review and Evidence Locker are designed so content can remain on your device.

You decide

You choose whether to save, delete, report, or share anything with a trusted person.

No invisible monitoring

Live Watch must be clearly enabled by you. It should never silently read a conversation.

Plain language

Privacy settings and limits should be understandable before someone uses the tool.

This is product-design copy for a prototype, not a final legal privacy policy. A production launch requires a reviewed policy, consent design, data-retention rules, and security controls.
