Terms and disclaimer

A safety aid, not a verdict.

Filtr. can highlight patterns and offer next steps. It cannot determine a person's intentions, replace professional care, or respond to emergencies.

Not law enforcement

Filtr. does not investigate, identify, track, or report people for you.

Not therapy or crisis care

It does not replace a mental-health professional, emergency service, trusted adult, or specialist support organisation.

Not proof

A signal is a prompt to pause and verify, not evidence that a person is a scammer.

Prototype notice

This site is a demonstrator. Production terms, privacy, safeguards, and support pathways require legal, security, and safeguarding review.
